§ 01 — About

Profile

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and cybersecurity. I have built secure applications across web and mobile, explored penetration testing workflows, written cryptography-heavy academic systems, and contributed to student-led technical communities through workshops and outreach.

Current interests concentrate on application security, the secure software development lifecycle, and the systems-level thinking needed to reason about real-world threat models. Much of my recent course work has drawn me toward mobile threat simulation and the design of defensible architectures.

I approach engineering the way I approach a research problem — by reading carefully, writing deliberately, and treating every system as something to be understood before it is changed.
