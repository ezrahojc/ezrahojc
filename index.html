§ 01 — About

Profile

My interests sit firmly on the offensive side of security. I enjoy the practical work of understanding how systems break — reconnaissance, web and mobile exploitation, privilege escalation, and the patient translation of a vulnerability into a working proof-of-concept. That orientation runs through my academic projects, my competition work, and the certifications I am pursuing.

Beyond penetration testing, I have built secure applications across web, mobile, and embedded platforms; written cryptography-heavy academic systems; and explored mobile threat simulation through the full pipeline — C2 infrastructure, payload capability, and R8/ProGuard obfuscation. I treat every system as something worth understanding before it is attacked or defended.

Outside coursework, I serve as Vice-President of SIT CYBERH4TS and as a TryHackMe Campus Champion, where I help run workshops, organise events, and grow student participation in the cyber community. I also work as a Student Technologist and Teaching Assistant at SIT, supporting undergraduates in web development and object-oriented programming.
